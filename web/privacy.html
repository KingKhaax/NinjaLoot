NinjaLoot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

NinjaLoot is a free, open source tool for World of Warcraft players that turns Raidbots simulations into charts. It is available as a Windows program, a portable program, a website and a Discord bot (“NinjaLoot”). This policy explains what data each part uses. The source code is public at github.com/KingKhaax/NinjaLoot, so anyone can check it.

What we do not do

NinjaLoot has no user accounts, does not collect telemetry or analytics, does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

The simulations you load, your settings and caches of item names and boss lists are stored only on your device (in the program's data folder, or in your browser's local storage for the website). You can delete them at any time from the program, by clearing your browser data or by uninstalling.

The NinjaLoot Discord bot

The bot only works in servers whose administrators invite it, and only reads channels it has permission to see. When a NinjaLoot user connects a channel, the bot server reads the new messages in that channel and returns only the codes of Raidbots report links found in them (for example the code in raidbots.com/simbot/report/…). Message text, authors, attachments and any other content are not stored, logged or shared. The server keeps a cache of the last few seconds in memory to avoid repeated requests, and nothing is written to disk. The bot does not send messages, does not use commands and does not access direct messages.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard request logs (IP address, time, requested URL) for a limited time for security and operation.

To stop the bot from reading a channel, turn off the Discord option in NinjaLoot, remove the bot's permissions on the channel, or remove the bot from your server.

Children

NinjaLoot is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by Discord in your country) and does not knowingly process their data.
